Shopify vs Etsy in the USA: What a $100 Order Really Costs

A $100 order is a good test of whether a marketplace is still the cheapest place to sell. At $48 or $60 the fee gap between Etsy and your own Shopify store is a few dollars; at $100 the gap is wide enough to change the decision. Here is the maths, with every figure from the published fee schedules.

On Etsy in the USA, a $100 order costs you four fees. The listing fee is $0.20. The transaction fee is 6.5% of the price including shipping: $6.50. Payment processing is 3% plus $0.25: $3.25. Add them up and Etsy keeps $9.95 of your $100 order, leaving you $90.05 before the cost of the product, packaging, and postage. If an Offsite Ad brought the buyer, add another 12% or 15% — $12.00 or $15.00 — and the sale shrinks to $78.05 or $75.05.

On your own Shopify store, the Basic plan costs $39 a month, and each order costs 2.9% plus $0.30 through Shopify Payments: $3.20 on a $100 order. That is $6.75 less per order than Etsy's $9.95. Divide the $39 monthly fee by that $6.75 saving and the crossover sits at roughly six orders a month. Sell more than six $100 orders a month and your own store starts winning on fees alone — before you count the control over branding, customer data, and repeat buyers that a marketplace never gives you.

The honest answer is not "Etsy is expensive" or "Shopify is always better". It depends on volume and order size. Under six orders a month, Etsy's per-order fees are cheaper than paying a monthly subscription. Above that, the subscription pays for itself — and the bigger your average order, the faster it pays, because Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee grows with every dollar while Shopify's monthly fee stays flat.
